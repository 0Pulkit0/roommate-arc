Shared basics

Questions worth asking

  1. When do we usually sleep, and when should the room be quiet?
  2. What food and supplies are shared, and what is off limits?
  3. How do we feel about visitors and people staying over?
  4. How are we splitting shared costs?
  5. Can we borrow each other's stuff, or is it ask first every time?
  6. What happens on exam nights when someone needs it totally quiet?
  7. If something is bothering us, how do we bring it up before it escalates?
