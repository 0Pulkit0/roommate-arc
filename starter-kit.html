Five questions worth asking

  1. When do we usually sleep, and when should the room be quiet?
  2. What food and supplies are shared, and what is off limits?
  3. What does "clean enough" actually mean to each of us?
  4. How do we feel about visitors and people staying over?
  5. How are we splitting shared costs?

You can like someone and still live really differently. Better to find out before you sign anything.
