About

Hi, I'm Pulkit.

I'm a first year student and this is one of my first websites. I made it for my web development end-term project using HTML and CSS, with a little bit of JavaScript on the starter kit page.

Why this site?

I watch a lot of anime, and I kept thinking about how some of my favourite characters would be really annoying to actually live with. So I turned that into a small joke website that reviews them like roommates, and added a packing checklist to make it useful too.

What I learned

How to link a few pages together with a shared navigation bar, how to lay things out with flexbox, how to make the pages look alright on a phone, and how to save checkbox ticks with a short JavaScript file.

My honest pick

I'd probably live with Sasuke. Quiet, tidy and he'd leave my stuff alone. I'd just have to accept that I'm having full conversations with myself.

Credits

The reviews are all my own jokes, not real facts. Characters belong to their creators: Naruto and Sasuke (Masashi Kishimoto), Luffy and Zoro (Eiichiro Oda), Gojo (Gege Akutami) and Levi (Hajime Isayama). This is an unofficial fan project.

Character pictures are fan art I found online. All credit to the original artists.
