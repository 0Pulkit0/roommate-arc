About

Hi, I'm Pulkit.

I'm a first year student and this is one of my first websites. I made it for my web development 1st term project using HTML and CSS.

Why this site?

I watch a lot of anime, and I kept thinking about how some of my favourite characters would be really annoying to actually live with. So I turned that into a small joke website that reviews them like roommates.

What I learned

tbh a lot, Flexbox handles most of the layout, one media query stops it breaking on a phone, and every page shares the same navigation bar. and most of my time actually went into getting the borders and shadows to sit right.
