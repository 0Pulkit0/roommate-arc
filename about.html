About

Hi, I'm Pulkit.

I'm a first year student and this is one of my first websites. I made it for my web development 1st term project using HTML and CSS.

Why this site?

I watch a lot of anime, and I kept thinking about how some of my favourite characters would be really annoying to actually live with. So I turned that into a small joke website that reviews them like roommates, and added a packing checklist to make it useful too.

What I learned

How to link a few pages together with a shared navigation bar, how to lay things out with flexbox, how to make the pages look alright on a phone, and how to build a simple checklist and suggestion form with plain HTML.

My honest pick

I'd probably live with Sasuke. Quiet, tidy and he'd leave my stuff alone. I'd just have to accept that I'm having full conversations with myself.
