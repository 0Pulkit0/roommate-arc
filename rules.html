The basics

It's a shared room. Act like it.

Six things worth agreeing on before small habits turn into long arguments.

  1. Your food is your food

    Ask before taking something you didn't buy. "I thought sharing is normal" is not true for everything.

  2. Headphones exist

    Agree on quiet hours. Your roommate dont need to hear the same reel six times in a row.

  3. Cleaning borrowed dishes after using them is the least thing anyone expects

    Wash what you used. A bowl rotting for three days is just a bowl someone will eventually have to wash.

  4. Mention your guests

    A quick heads up before someone stays over is basic courtesy. It's their room too.

  5. Pay your share

    Agree on shared costs and outings. Nobody wants to become the debt collector that too from their friends.

  6. Answer the practical stuff and Ask before using other's stuff

    You don't have to be best friends but you do need to say whether you're buying something this week.
