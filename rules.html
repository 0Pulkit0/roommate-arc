The basics

It's a shared room. Act like it.

Six things worth agreeing on before small habits turn into really long arguments.

  1. Your food is your food

    Ask before taking something you didn't buy. "I thought it was shared" gets old fast.

  2. Headphones exist

    Agree on quiet hours. Your roommate doesn't need to hear the same reel six times in a row.

  3. Leave the kitchen usable

    Wash what you used. A pan "soaking" for three days is just a pan someone else will end up washing.

  4. Mention your guests

    A quick heads-up before someone stays over is basic courtesy. It's their room too.

  5. Pay your share

    Agree on shared costs and dates. Nobody wants to become the household debt collector.

  6. Answer the practical stuff

    You don't have to be best friends. You do need to say whether you're buying the bin bags this week.

Agree on the rules together

Everyone has different standards. Say yours out loud before deciding someone should have just known. Start the conversation.
